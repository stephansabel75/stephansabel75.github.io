2. Overview

Marblematic is a puzzle game for Android. There are no user accounts and no servers of our own. We do not collect, store or transmit any personal data ourselves. The game is funded by ads from Google AdMob; Google processes data as described below.

3. Data on your device

Game progress, settings (sound, volume, vibration, language), boosters and lives are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

If Android backup is turned on, Android backs up this save file to your Google account so you can restore it on a new device. This backup is performed by Google under your Android settings and can be turned off there.

4. Advertising with Google AdMob

Ads (banner and optional rewarded videos) are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google").

Data that may be sent to Google includes your device's advertising ID, IP address (and an approximate location derived from it), device and app information (e.g. model, operating system, language) and information about your interaction with ads. Google uses this data to serve ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and — only with your consent — personalise ads.

Consent: On first launch the app asks for your consent through Google's consent platform (UMP) where required in your region. Personalised ads are only shown with your consent; if you decline, the app shows non-personalised ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Google may transfer data to the United States; Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings under Google → Ads.

5. Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google Play's privacy terms apply to downloads, updates and crash reports.
